# Demo Video Script — AI Receptionist for Dental Clinics (60 seconds)

**Setup:** open the public demo page (`/`), start screen recording, keep the phone
widget centered. Speak naturally — timings are approximate.

---

**[0:00–0:08] Hook**

> "What happens when a patient calls your clinic at 11 PM? Usually — nothing.
> The call goes to voicemail, and by morning they've booked with someone else.
> This is Nora, an AI receptionist that answers every call, day or night."

*Action: widget visible, greeting message on screen.*

**[0:08–0:30] Booking flow**

> "Watch — I'm the caller. I want a cleaning."

*Action: tap "📅 Book appointment" → tap "Cleaning" → type a name → type a phone
number → pick a day → pick a time → tap "Confirm booking".*

> "Name, phone, day, time — booked in under 30 seconds, with an SMS confirmation
> on the way. No staff needed."

**[0:30–0:45] Emergency escalation**

> "Now the important part — emergencies. If a caller says…"

*Action: tap "↺ New call", then type: "I have severe tooth pain and swelling".*

> "Nora detects the emergency instantly, connects them to the dentist, flags it
> urgent in the system, and offers the next urgent slot. A real emergency never
> waits on hold."

*Action: tap "Yes, book urgent slot" → type name → pick day/time → confirm.
Point at the 🚨 URGENT badge on the confirmation.*

**[0:45–0:55] Dashboard**

> "And everything lands here — the clinic dashboard. Every booking, every call,
> every emergency, with full transcripts."

*Action: switch to `/admin`, scroll the bookings list and call log, expand one
transcript.*

**[0:55–1:00] Close**

> "This is a concept demo — but the same system can plug into your real booking
> software and phone line. If missed calls are costing you patients, let's talk."

---

**Recording tips**

- Do the full run once off-camera to warm up the localStorage state, then
  "Clear demo data" (admin page) right before recording for a clean take.
- Keep the browser at ~1280px wide; the widget stays centered.
- Type at a natural pace — the AI "typing…" indicator adds realism, don't rush it.
- For the emergency line, type it exactly: `I have severe tooth pain and swelling`
